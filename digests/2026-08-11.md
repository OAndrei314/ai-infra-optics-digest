### $\texttt{DisMorph}$: learning to disentangle technical distortions from true biological change

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-11
- Tags: `ai_compute`
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08173

Existing registration methods estimate a single field that conflates biological and technical effects, potentially biasing downstream morphometric measurements if distortions remain (partially) uncorrected. We propose $\texttt{DisMorph}$, a registration framework trained entirely on synthetic data that explicitly decomposes longitudinal deformation into technical and anatomical transforms.
