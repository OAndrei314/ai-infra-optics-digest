### A Conditional No-Hair Theorem for Transformers: How Language Models Forget Their Prompt

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-18
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24133
- Matched layers: ai_compute, data_center_energy

We are inspired to make the analogy and we ask how much an autoregressive transformer retains of its prompt after generating $t$ tokens. For two prompts followed by the same continuation, the difference of their hidden states decays approximately as a power law $t^{-\alpha}$, where $\alpha=1$ is the rate at which a fixed prompt's share of uniform attention shrinks.
