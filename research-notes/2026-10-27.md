### "Not" is Not Lost in Translation: A Causal Analysis of Negation Representations in Multilingual LLMs

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32281
- Matched layers: ai_compute

Despite this, multilingual large language models (LLMs) translate negation robustly, raising the question of whether they share an underlying representation of negation across languages. ### $M^3$: A Multi-LLM System for Multi-Turn Jailbreaking as a Markov Decision Process

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31937
- Matched layers: market_context

We address this gap by introducing $M^3$, an automated red-teaming framework that formalizes multi-turn jailbreaking as a finite-horizon Markov Decision Process (MDP). Across two benchmarks and six target models, $M^3$ consistently outperforms state-of-the-art multi-turn red-teaming methods in Attack Success Rates (ASR), achieving improvements of up to 53% in ASR@5.
