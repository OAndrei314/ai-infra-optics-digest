### A Physics-Guided Transformer Framework for Electromigration Analysis in Multi-Segment Interconnects

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-07
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06464
- Matched layers: data_center_energy

Accurate transient stress analysis is needed to identify wires susceptible to EM degradation, but applying physics-based solvers across many interconnects remains computationally expensive. This paper proposes a physics-guided transformer framework for fast EM stress prediction in multi-segment interconnect lines.

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14858
